Recording HTML Decks

Slides and narration in one file, recorded with vac html video.

Where Narration Lives

  • A data-vo attribute on each .slide
  • Or a child <aside class="notes">
  • [PAUSE:ms] adds silence after the slide

Speaker Notes Work Too

This slide has no data-vo. Its narration comes from the notes element below, as in reveal.js.
